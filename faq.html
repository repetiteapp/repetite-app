Getting started

Is it actually free, or will it ask me to pay after a week?

It's free for good. Logging food and workouts, barcode scanning, the rest timer, reminders and widgets never cost anything, and there are no ads. Pro is optional: unlimited AI, your full history and smarter targets.

Do I have to make an account?

No. Open it and start logging. Sign in only if you want a cloud backup.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Repetite is coming to the App Store first.

When can I download it?

Soon, on the App Store. Want a heads-up? Email repetiteapp@gmail.com.

Is there a free trial of Pro, and can I cancel?

The yearly plan starts with a free week. Cancel any time in your App Store settings and you won't be charged again.

Is this medical advice?

No. It helps you track and plan, but it's not a doctor or dietitian. Check with a doctor before a big change. More in the Terms of Use.

Food and training

Can I log Indian food like dal, roti and paneer?

Yes. Plenty of Indian dishes are built in, and you can type amounts like "2 roti" or "dal 200 g".

How accurate is logging a meal from a photo?

It's a good estimate, not a lab test. You see every food and portion and can fix anything before it's saved.

Does it work without internet?

Mostly. Food search, logging and workouts all work offline. Only the AI features need a connection.

I'm new to the gym. Will it tell me what to do?

Yes. Answer five questions and it builds you a plan. Pro also gives you the weight and reps to aim for on every set.

Can I use it just for food, or just for the gym?

Yes. Use the half you need. The other half stays out of your way.

Can I bring my history from Hevy or Strong?

Yes. Export it there, import it here, and your past lifts and records come with it.

Does it work with my Apple Watch, Fitbit or Garmin?

Yes, through Apple Health. Sleep, heart rate, steps and calories come in on their own.

Reminders and your data

Will it spam me with notifications?

No. A reminder only comes when something's missing, and never in your quiet hours. You choose which kinds you get.

Who can see my data? Do you sell it?

Your logs stay on your device. We don't sell data, and there are no ad trackers. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What happens if I get a new phone or delete the app?

Without an account, deleting the app deletes your logs. Sign in for a daily cloud backup and a new phone gets everything back.
