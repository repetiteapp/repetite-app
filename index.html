FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Repetite free?

Yes. Logging food and workouts, the food database, barcode scanning, the rest timer, widgets, Siri and Apple Health are all free, with no ads. Pro adds next-session targets, weekly calorie check-ins, unlimited AI and your full history. Pro is billed through your Apple ID and you can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. You can sign in with your email if you'd like a cloud backup, so a new phone can get everything back.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you allow AI, only what each answer needs is sent to the AI service through Repetite's server. If you sign in, your backups are stored privately for your account. The Privacy Policy lists everything.

Does it work with Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin or Oura?

Yes, through Apple Health. Apple Watch works on its own; for other devices, turn on Apple Health in the device's app and Repetite picks up your sleep, heart rate, steps and calories burned.

Can I bring my workouts from another app?

Yes. Export your history from Hevy or Strong and import it in Repetite. Your past weights and records carry over, so suggestions work from day one.

Does it work for vegetarian and Indian diets?

Yes. The food database includes Indian dishes, and you can tell Repetite you're vegetarian, vegan or eat eggs, so food ideas from the coach fit how you eat.

Is this medical advice?

No. Repetite helps you track and plan, but it isn't medical, nutritional or fitness advice from a professional. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or training program. See the Terms of Use.
