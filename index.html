    FAQ

    Things people ask

    Is it actually free, or will it ask me to pay after a week?

    It's free for good. Logging food and workouts, barcode scanning, the rest timer, reminders and widgets never cost anything, and there are no ads. Pro is optional: unlimited AI, your full history and smarter targets.

    Do I have to make an account?

    No. Open it and start logging. Sign in only if you want a cloud backup.

    Can I log Indian food like dal, roti and paneer?

    Yes. Plenty of Indian dishes are built in, and you can type amounts like "2 roti" or "dal 200 g".

    When can I download it?

    Soon, on the App Store. Want a heads-up? Email repetiteapp@gmail.com.
