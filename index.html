A day with Repetite

From breakfast to your last set, in one app.

Most people use one app for food and another for the gym. Repetite does both, and each side knows what the other is doing.

Your whole day on one screen.

Open the app and see what's left to eat, what you're training, and how you slept, before you've scrolled.

  • What's left to eatCalories, protein, carbs and fat, counting down as you log.
  • Today's workout, one tap awayStart it from Today, or see when your next session is.
  • Your watch, built inSleep, resting heart rate, steps and calories burned from Apple Health.
  • A streak that keeps you goingDays logged in a row, right at the top.

Log a meal in the time it takes to eat a bite.

Type it the way you'd say it, scan the packet or take a photo. Repetite finds the food and works out the portion.

  • 12,774 foods, even offlineType "2 eggs" or "rice 150 g" and the amount is understood. Indian dishes included.
  • Scan a barcodePackaged food from Open Food Facts, saved for next time.
  • Snap a meal, or say itAI reads the plate or what you said, and fills in the numbers for you to check.
  • AI when it isn't in the databaseAn estimate for anything else, saved so you never ask twice.

Every meal, every macro, at a glance.

Your day by meal, with what each food added. Fix a portion in a tap, or log yesterday's lunch again.

  • Meals in the order you ate themBreakfast to dinner, with the time and the totals.
  • Vitamins and mineralsSee what your day adds up to, not just calories.
  • Meal plansSave a good day and put it on any other day.
  • Your usual, in one tapRecent foods and saved meals, logged again without searching.

A target for every set.

Repetite remembers what you lifted last time and suggests what to beat today, so every session moves you forward.

  • Next-session targetsThe weight and reps to aim for on every lift, from your own history.
  • A rest timer that rings on timeWith a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Plates for each sideLoad the bar without the maths.
  • Warm-ups, drop sets, supersetsAnd reps left in the tank, for the way you really train.

See where you're heading, and when you'll get there.

Each week Repetite compares your weight trend with your plan and suggests new calories when you stall.

  • Your weight trend and goal dateA steady line through the daily ups and downs.
  • Weekly check-insCalories that follow your real results.
  • Strength that adds upYour estimated best on every lift, and how it's moving.
  • Every day on a calendarTap any day for its meals and workout.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is Repetite free?

    Yes. Logging food and workouts, the food database, barcode scanning, the rest alarm, smart reminders, widgets, Siri and Apple Health are all free, with no ads. Pro adds next-session targets, weekly calorie check-ins, unlimited AI and your full history. Pro is billed through your Apple ID and you can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings.

    Will it spam me with notifications?

    No. Reminders only come when something's missing, at the times you usually eat and train. There are never more than three a day, nothing during your quiet hours, and you choose which kinds you get in Settings → Notifications.

    Does the rest timer work with my phone locked?

    Yes. When rest is over your iPhone rings like a Clock timer, even on silent, and the countdown sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with −15 s, +15 s and Skip.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Everything works without one. You can sign in with your email if you'd like a cloud backup, so a new phone can get everything back.

    Where is my data stored?

    On your iPhone. If you allow AI, only what each answer needs is sent to the AI service through Repetite's server. If you sign in, your backups are stored privately for your account. The Privacy Policy lists everything.

    Does it work with Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin or Oura?

    Yes, through Apple Health. Apple Watch works on its own; for other devices, turn on Apple Health in the device's app and Repetite picks up your sleep, heart rate, steps and calories burned.

    Can I bring my workouts from another app?

    Yes. Export your history from Hevy or Strong and import it in Repetite. Your past weights and records carry over, so suggestions work from day one.

    Does it work for vegetarian and Indian diets?

    Yes. The food database includes Indian dishes, and you can tell Repetite you're vegetarian, vegan or eat eggs, so food ideas fit how you eat.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. Repetite helps you track and plan, but it isn't medical, nutritional or fitness advice from a professional. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or training program. See the Terms of Use.
