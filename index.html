A day with Repetite

Breakfast to last set, same app

You probably track food in one app and lifts in another, and neither has a clue about the other. Repetite puts both in one place, so you can see what's left to eat and what you're training without switching apps.

1234 12 12 1234 123

Open it and you know where you stand

Calories left, today's workout and last night's sleep are all on the first screen. No digging around.

  • 1What's left to eatCalories, protein, carbs and fat. The numbers drop as you log.
  • 2Today's workoutStart it right here, or check when the next one is.
  • 3Numbers from your watchSleep, resting heart rate, steps and calories burned, pulled in from Apple Health.
  • 4Your streakHow many days in a row you've logged. Try not to break it.

Logging food shouldn't take longer than eating it

Type it like you'd text a friend, scan the packet, or take a photo of your plate. Repetite finds the food and works out the portion.

  • 112,000+ foods, works offlineType "2 eggs" or "rice 150 g" and it gets the amount. Indian dishes are in there too.
  • 2Photo or voiceAI reads your plate or what you said. You check the numbers before anything's saved.
  • Barcode scannerLooks up packaged food on Open Food Facts and remembers it for next time.
  • Something unusual?AI makes an estimate and saves it, so next time it's already in your list.

Your day, meal by meal

See what each food added. Fix a portion with a tap, or copy yesterday's lunch.

  • 1In the order you ateBreakfast through dinner, with times and totals.
  • 2Vitamins and mineralsIron, calcium, vitamin D and the rest, added up for the day.
  • Meal plansHad a good day? Save it and reuse it whenever you like.
  • Your usualRecent foods and saved meals, logged again without searching.

Every set has a number to beat

Repetite remembers your last session and tells you what to aim for this time. Hit it, and next time asks for a little more.

  • 1Next-session targetsWeight and reps for every lift, worked out from your own history.
  • 2A rest timer that actually ringsIt counts down on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • 3Plates per sideNo more mental maths with a loaded bar.
  • 4Warm-ups, drop sets, supersetsPlus reps left in the tank, if you track that.

Where you're heading, and roughly when

Every week Repetite checks your weight trend against your plan. Stalled? It suggests new calories.

  • 1Weight trend and goal dateA smooth line, so one salty dinner doesn't wreck your week.
  • 2Weekly check-insCalorie targets based on what's actually happening.
  • 3Strength over timeYour estimated best on each lift, and which way it's going.
  • A calendar of everythingTap any day to see what you ate and lifted.

    FAQ

    Things people ask

    Is Repetite free?

    Yes. Logging food and workouts, the food database, the barcode scanner, the rest alarm, smart reminders, widgets, Siri and Apple Health are all free, and there are no ads. Pro adds next-session targets, weekly calorie check-ins, unlimited AI and your full history. It's billed through your Apple ID, and you can cancel any time in your device's Settings.

    Will it spam me with notifications?

    No. A reminder only comes when something's actually missing, around the time you usually eat or train. Do the thing and it's cancelled. They're at least an hour apart, your quiet hours are left alone, and you choose which kinds you get in Settings → Notifications.

    Do I need an account?

    No, everything works without one. Sign in with your email only if you want a cloud backup, so a new device can get everything back.
