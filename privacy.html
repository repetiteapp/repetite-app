Repetite

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

The short version.

Your food, workout and weight logs are stored on your iPhone. You don't need an account.

When you use an AI feature, only what that answer needs is sent to the AI service, through our server.

No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains what the Repetite app ("Repetite", "we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Repetite is made by Mukund Mangla. Questions: repetiteapp@gmail.com.

1. Data that stays on your iPhone

Everything you log in Repetite is saved on your device: meals and foods, workouts and sets, body weight, water, habits, skincare, your profile (name, age, sex, height, goals and targets) and your settings. We can't see it unless you choose one of the features below that sends something.

Repetite's widgets and Siri Shortcuts read the same on-device data. Reminders and the rest-timer alert are scheduled on your iPhone.

2. Apple Health

If you allow it, Repetite reads sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps and calories burned, and writes your body weight, finished workouts and logged meals. You choose exactly what to share in the iPhone's Health settings, and you can change it any time.

Health data is never used for advertising and never sold. It leaves your iPhone only in the two ways described below: summaries of sleep and heart rate in an AI coach question (section 3), and your own cloud backup if you sign in (section 4).

3. AI features

AI is optional and off until you allow it. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Account. When it's on:

FeatureWhat's sentWho answers
Food search and estimatesThe food you typed, and its amountGoogle (Gemini API)
Meal photosThe photo you choose to analyse, and any note you addGoogle (Gemini API)
AI coach and daily briefingYour question; your first name, goal, targets and weight; a summary of your recent food and training; and sleep and heart rate if Apple Health is connectedGroq, or Google (Gemini API) when Groq is busy

These requests go through our server, which adds no personal details: your email address is never sent to an AI service. The AI services process the request to produce the answer, under their own terms (Google, Groq). AI answers can be wrong; see our Terms.

To keep AI usage fair, our server counts how many AI requests each install makes per day. For that, the app creates an anonymous account identifier the first time you use AI. It isn't linked to your name or email.

4. Your account and cloud backup (optional)

You can use Repetite without an account. If you sign in with your email address, we store:

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • Cloud backups: a copy of your Repetite data (including anything above that came from Apple Health), so you can restore it on a new phone. We keep your newest three backups. Only your account can read your backups through the app.

Accounts and backups are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase.

5. Purchases

Repetite Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment: we never see your card or payment details. When you subscribe, the app sends Apple's signed record of the subscription (which plan, purchase and renewal dates, and Apple's transaction numbers) to our server, linked to your Repetite account identifier, so we know which accounts have Pro. Apple also sends us notifications when a subscription renews, ends or is refunded.

6. Barcode lookups

When you scan a product, its barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up its nutrition. Nothing else is sent with it.

7. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics trackers in the app.
  • No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data.

If you've allowed it on your iPhone, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us through App Store Connect.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your iPhone: deleting the app deletes its data.
  • Your account: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and your cloud backups straight away. Subscription records stay for our accounts (Apple requires us to keep sales records) but are no longer linked to you.
  • Anything else, including the anonymous AI usage counts: email us and we'll delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how it's used. Email repetiteapp@gmail.com; we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

In India, this email is also our grievance contact under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If we don't resolve your complaint, you can take it to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Repetite is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child under 13 has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

Data sent to our server travels encrypted (HTTPS). Server access is limited to what each feature needs, and each account can reach only its own backups.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
